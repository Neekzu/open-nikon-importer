# Privacy

Open Nikon Importer is designed as a local-only camera import utility.

## What The App Does

- Reads the connected camera catalog through macOS camera APIs.
- Imports selected media files into a local folder.
- Reads local preview/proxy files when available.

## What The App Does Not Do

- No account login.
- No analytics.
- No telemetry.
- No background upload.
- No cloud sync.
- No deletion from the camera by default.

Future auto-update support may contact a public update feed to check for new releases. That should be documented before it ships.

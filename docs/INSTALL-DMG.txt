OPEN NIKON IMPORTER

Install:

1. Drag "Open Nikon Importer.app" onto "Applications".
2. Open Applications.
3. Open "Open Nikon Importer".

If macOS says it cannot verify the app:

1. Open Applications.
2. Right-click "Open Nikon Importer".
3. Click Open.
4. Confirm Open once.

When macOS asks for removable-media access, click Allow.

The app imports files locally and does not delete files from your camera.
